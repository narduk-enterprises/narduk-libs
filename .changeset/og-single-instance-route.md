Accept a parameterized social-preview route with one sample when it states a
`reason`. Two samples are what prove a dynamic preview varies with its
parameter, but a route family that genuinely has one instance today — one
published state, one live tenant — cannot supply a second real path, and an
invented one proves nothing. Routes with no reason still require two.
