`NardukLineChart` `bands` prop (`ChartBand`): a filled band between a `low` and a `high` series that vary along X, such as a day-of-year 25th to 75th percentile normal range. Drawn behind the grid and lines in a low-opacity theme-token colour, broken by a `null` in either edge, part of the Y domain, legend, tooltip and data table.
